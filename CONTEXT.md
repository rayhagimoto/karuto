# Capture overlay

The capture overlay lets a person choose what to capture and how to capture it.

## Language

**Capture mode**:
Whether a capture produces a Snapshot or a Record.

**Snapshot**:
A capture mode that produces a still image.

**Record**:
A capture mode that produces a screen recording over time.

**Area selection mode**:
The kind of screen target selected for capture: Area, Fullscreen, Window, or Scrolling. Scrolling is available for Snapshot only.

**Group 1**:
The overlay toolbar group for choosing the Capture mode: Snapshot or Record.

**Group 2**:
The overlay toolbar group for choosing the Area selection mode.

**Group 3**:
The overlay toolbar group containing the timer, capture options, and cancel controls.
